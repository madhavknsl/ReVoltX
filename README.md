ReVoltX — Powering the Second Life of Energy

ReVoltX is building a new ecosystem for EV Battery Second-Life Economics—transforming retired EV batteries into valuable, intelligent, and reliable energy assets.

Instead of treating an EV battery as waste once its automotive life ends, we test, diagnose, grade, repurpose, and redeploy its remaining energy potential across multiple applications—from telecom backup and solar storage to commercial energy systems and microgrids.

Our vision goes beyond battery reuse. We aim to build the infrastructure, technology, and marketplace that connects retired batteries with their next best use, creating value for EV manufacturers, fleet operators, energy companies, and end users.

By extending battery life before recycling, ReVoltX aims to reduce energy-storage costs, minimize resource waste, accelerate the circular economy, and turn every retired battery into a new energy opportunity.

We don't believe a battery has an expiry date.
We believe it has a next chapter.

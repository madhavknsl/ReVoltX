The Gap

Retired isn't dead. But India treats it that way.

Most retired packs go straight to a scrap dealer or an informal dismantler, priced by weight, not by what they can still do. Six things are missing between "retired" and "recycled" — ReVoltX builds all six.

01

No health verification

Priced by age or weight, never by measured condition.

02

No certification

No standard record of capacity, safety, or history follows the pack.

03

No warranty or insurance

A repurposed cell that fails has nowhere to point the liability.

04

No trusted buyer network

Telecom, solar, and charging operators have no safe way to source used cells.

05

No organized logistics

Collection is ad hoc — batteries go to whoever pays cash first.

06

Value lost to informal channels

Scrap dealers under-price residual capacity and under-deliver on safety.

The Process

Six decisions. One certified second life.

01
Source

OEMs, fleets, and service centres flag retired packs.

02
Collect

Reverse logistics moves packs to a regional hub.

03
Certify & Grade

AI + BMS diagnostics assign a trust grade.

04
Repurpose

Healthy modules rebuilt with a new BMS and enclosure.

05
Deploy & Match

AI matches the asset to its buyer.

06
Recycle

Only end-of-life cells reach material recovery.

Safety gate at step 3: swollen, leaking, punctured, or thermally compromised packs never enter reuse — they're routed straight to certified recycling.
